# VASU-140M source-policy approval

On 2026-08-02, the VASU project owner approved use of the proposed FineWeb-Edu
extension under ODC-By 1.0 and of the planned English Wikipedia source under
CC-BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL, subject to their recorded attribution, notice,
redistribution, and ShareAlike obligations.

The owner also approved use of the assistant-authored internal evaluation suite
for internal source-admission evidence only. It remains non-independent and
must not support external benchmark or independent-held-out claims.

This is source-policy approval only. It does not approve acquisition, source
admission, data construction, publication, evaluation execution, checkpoint
creation, or training.
