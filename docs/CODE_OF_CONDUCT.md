# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in VASU welcoming and respectful for everyone.

---

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful.
* Be constructive.
* Be open to feedback.
* Help others learn.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Discrimination
* Trolling
* Spam

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this code of conduct.

---

## Philosophy

VASU exists to learn, build, and share knowledge.

Treat others with kindness and professionalism.
